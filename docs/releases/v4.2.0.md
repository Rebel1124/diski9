# Diski 9 v4.2.0 — Settlement, voids and corrections

**Release date:** 20 November 2026. **Status:** stable development milestone,
accepted by the product owner (Phase 6C.4, Stages A–G). **Not production-ready and
not deployed publicly.**

## What this release is

Confirmed Weekly Challenge entries are now **settled** from recorded result
evidence under the approved `settle/v1` policy, with voids, round invalidation,
audited corrections, finalisation and owner-only reopening, and participants see
their actual results on My Challenge. Builds on v4.1.0 (Phases 6C.1–6C.3).

### Result evidence and settlement

* **Policy (decisions S-01 to S-11):** completion-based settlement cutoff with no
  grace period; two-branch early-start rule (V3) with the provider's first-half time
  as supporting evidence only; conflicting results kept, never silently replaced;
  24-hour correction window measured from the last material change; owner-only
  reopening of finalised rounds; explicit development result modes.
* **Migration `app_0006`** (additive): insert-only result observations (provider,
  operator, synthetic) with receipt-time provenance and repeated states, verdict
  rules and evidence, settlement-run manifests, settlement versions, alerts and
  their audited resolutions, a frozen correction window and result mode per round.
  Downgrade is refused once settlement data exists (settlement history is never
  discarded).
* **By-ID provider results import** (up to 20 fixtures per request, only fixtures
  selected in locked entries); existing football ingestion unchanged.
* **Settlement** through the unchanged quantitative engine: void predictions at
  odds 1.00 (D-32), half-up rounding (D-15), all-nine-void refunds of 100.00 that
  are not rank-eligible (D-33), round invalidation with competitive and practice
  variants (D-32b), partial settlement with "settled so far" totals; one
  transaction per round, idempotent, reconstructible from its evidence manifest.
* **Corrections, finalisation, reopening:** impact previews and confirmation tokens
  (stale previews refused), mandatory reasons and evidence, immutable audit
  records, blocking alerts that need an audited resolution, finalisation after the
  correction window, owner-only reopening with a new settlement version.

### Operator command line

* `results`, `settle` and `round invalidate / finalise-check / finalise / reopen`
  commands; development-only result scenarios (15) and test entries.
* **Every settlement command verifies the acting person's Supabase password**
  interactively (hidden, from a terminal only; never an argument, variable or
  piped input); roles come from the database; database access is not authority;
  recent-authentication rules apply at the moment of confirmation.

### My Challenge

* The participant's own result in the existing cards: status (locked, partly
  settled, settled with the round check pending, provisional, final, corrected,
  reopened, refunded, round void), allocated / returned / net credits, prediction
  counts, recovery, the four accumulators with their actual returns, and each
  prediction's frozen odds, official 90-minute score, verdict and void reason.
* Unresolved payouts are never shown as zero; recovery and net are not decided
  before the entry and the round's validity check are complete; refunds are never
  shown as recoveries. Pre-match Market, Diski 9 and Your View estimates are kept
  and labelled as expectations.
* Withdrawal now clears the withdrawn portfolio's saved predictions (a fresh
  start; T-09 clarified).

## What this release is not

* **No leaderboards, weekly ranks, points or championship standings** on real
  entries (Phase 6C.5); the championship panel and Leaderboards still use
  demonstration data.
* **No administration interface, automated scheduling or monitoring**; results are
  imported and rounds settled, finalised and published with operator commands
  (Phase 6C.6).
* **Not deployed.** Runs locally; no hosted service or private beta (Phase 10).

## Unchanged

Goal Model 1.0, the Screener and Match Lab analytics, Portfolio Analysis, odds
ingestion and consensus, forecast snapshots, the SQLite football schema and
ingestion, the independent research collector and its schedules, and the approved
competition mathematics (`broad60-432-v1`, D-15 rounding, `points-150-75-v2`).

* **Automated tests (release baseline):** **1,481 passed, 0 failed, 0 skipped** (real PostgreSQL, local

  type passwords into the real command.
* **CI:** green on the accepted branch (run 48 on the final code; the closure commits are documentation only); lint and format clean. Post-merge result on `main`: see the release commit and the tag annotation.
* **Quantitative regression:** fingerprint SHA-256 `88bcd717deca6c1c…`,
  byte-identical to v4.1.0; engine, ingestion, research and odds modules unchanged.
* **Live provider check:** 3 of 5 authorised requests; multi-ID contract confirmed.
* **Migrations and restart:** fresh install; upgrade from `app_0005` with existing
  data unchanged; downgrade refusal leaves settlement data intact; a PostgreSQL
  restart left all settlement data identical.
* **Browser journeys** (desktop, tablet, mobile): 12 settlement journeys plus the
  current-round view; no overflow, console or page errors.
* **Product owner's local acceptance** on the Mac: upgrade with backup, operator
  password checks, partial and complete settlement, correction, finalisation,
  reopening and invalidation; two issues found were fixed before acceptance.
