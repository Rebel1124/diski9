# Diski 9 v4.1.0 — Competition backend and persistence

**Release date:** 19 November 2026. **Status:** stable development milestone,
accepted by the product owner (Phase 6C.3). **Not production-ready and not
deployed publicly.**

## What this release is

The Weekly Challenge moves from a browser-session prototype (v4.0.0) to a
server-backed competition with real accounts and permanent, authoritative
portfolios. It covers Phases 6C.1 to 6C.3 and the Match Lab guidance work that
preceded them.

### Application infrastructure (6C.1)

* PostgreSQL 17 application database (`app` schema, SQLAlchemy 2 Core), separate
  from the SQLite football store; local Docker Compose setup.
* Alembic migrations `app_0001`–`app_0005` (competition schema, authentication,
  competition persistence, contextual Your View, writer order).
* Security protections: the private `app` schema is not exposed to browser roles;
  Supabase-managed auth schemas are never changed by application migrations;
  database constraints and triggers enforce set-once publication fields,
  insert-only snapshots and submission history, and deadlines.
* Repositories for accounts, rounds, portfolios, jobs and audit; CI with a
  PostgreSQL service.

### Accounts and permissions (6C.2)

* Supabase Auth sign-up, sign-in, email verification and recovery, used by the
  server only; no passwords stored in the Diski 9 database; no privileged keys in
  the browser.
* Public usernames and anonymity: other participants see a username, never an
  email address or name.
* Server-side sessions and permissions: a server-built principal carries
  capabilities; no browser-supplied role, editable profile data or untrusted
  identity-provider metadata can grant elevated permissions.
* Account pages, account menu and guest/account isolation.

### Persistent competition (6C.3)

* Persistent competition rounds with **immutable published snapshots** (fixtures,
  offers and probabilities frozen at publication, with fingerprints).
* Standard publication target **Friday 08:00 SAST**; deadline 15 minutes before
  the earliest kickoff.
* **Persistent portfolio drafts** with optimistic versions and stale-edit
  protection (per-tab writer tokens and sequence numbers).
* **Contextual Your View**: separate live and frozen-round adjustments, private and
  never scored.
* **Guest-work adoption** on sign-in, by the participant's explicit choice.
* **Confirmation, replacement, withdrawal and re-entry**, with authoritative
  validation of the strict 4/3/2 structure and 40/30/20/10 credits.
* **Immutable confirmed submission history** (insert-only submissions and events).
* **Server-authoritative deadline enforcement**: checked inside the transaction,
  after the round locks, against the database clock.
* **Practice versus competitive** round classification (competitive needs at least
  five valid entries at the deadline).
* **Most recent locked entry** on My Challenge, read-only and owner-scoped.
* Concurrency protections: round share/update locks, idempotency keys, one
  current submission per entry.

### Experience

* Match Lab educational guidance: static factor guidance beside the team controls
  replaces free-text reasons and research notes (D-41); layout refinements and a
  two-row mobile header.
* Updated My Challenge: compact, even "This round" and "Confirmed portfolio" cards;
  Market, Diski 9 and Your View (calculated now, private, never scored) as three
  same-size estimate cards.

## What this release is not

* **No settlement.** Results are not yet settled; no verdicts, payouts, voids or
  corrections (Phase 6C.4).
* **No live leaderboards or championship scoring** on real entries (Phase 6C.5);
  leaderboards still use demonstration data.
* **No administration interface or automated scheduling**; rounds are published
  and locked with operator commands (Phase 6C.6).
* **Not deployed.** Runs locally (PostgreSQL in Docker, local Supabase Auth); no
  hosted or public service, no private beta (Phase 10).

## Unchanged

Goal Model 1.0 (k_s 12, k_t 96, m0 30), the Screener and Match Lab analytics, odds
ingestion, strict consensus and freshness rules, Edge/EV, forecast snapshots, the
SQLite football schema, and the approved competition mathematics
(`broad60-432-v1`, D-15 rounding, `points-150-75-v2`).

## Validation

* **Automated tests:** 1,304 passed, 1 skipped (real PostgreSQL and local Supabase
  Auth). The skip needs a database holding a real hosted Supabase `auth` schema;
  hosted compatibility testing is deferred to Phase 10.
* **Quantitative regression:** engine code byte-identical to the pre-6C.3
  baseline; the comparative numerical fingerprint is byte-identical; engine suites
  407/407 on both versions.
* **Migrations and restart:** fresh install, upgrades from `app_0002` and
  `app_0003`, downgrade and re-upgrade; a real PostgreSQL restart left all data
  identical.
* **Browser journeys A–H** (desktop, tablet and mobile): no overflow, no page
  errors.
* **Product owner's local testing:** migrations, owner bootstrap, development and
  real rounds, portfolio confirmation, replacement and persistence, and the
  approved My Challenge layout. The optional local lock check was not run by the
  owner; lock is covered by automated tests and the browser journeys.
* **CI** green on the accepted branch; lint and format clean. Post-merge results on
  `main`: see the release commit message and the tag annotation.

## Known limitations (accepted, deferred)

Full history browsing (6C.5); periodic Screener reload latency and direct
`/?fixture=` selection (6D); command-line tracebacks for expected errors (6C.6);
hosted Supabase testing (Phase 10); freezing Your View at confirmation (T-21
Option B, needs a D-41 decision). D-29 (scoring validation on real pools) and D-36
(account deletion and retention) remain outstanding requirements.
