# Diski 9 beta terms (draft, version beta-2026-11)

**Draft for the closed beta. Subject to legal review before any public launch.**

1. Diski 9 is an educational football analytics product. The Weekly Challenge uses
   virtual credits only. Credits have no monetary value, cannot be bought, sold or
   exchanged, and no prizes are offered.
2. You must be 18 or older to create an account.
3. Probabilities, estimates and reference odds are modelled information, not
   guarantees and not betting advice. Reference odds are not offers.
4. Choose a username that does not identify you if you prefer, and that is not
   offensive or misleading. We may change usernames that break these rules.
5. Your username (or, if you choose anonymity, a random alias) may appear on
   public leaderboards. Your email address is never shown publicly.
6. Do not attempt to access other people's accounts, interfere with the service or
   automate entries.
7. Account deletion and data-retention rules will be published before launch.
